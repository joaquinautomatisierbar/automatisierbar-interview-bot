# Patrik's Owned Surface — the Sandbox Lead-Ops Digest

*This is the motivation engine of the whole program. One real thing that is genuinely **his**. Ownership is what turns 3–5h into 10–15h — not tracking. Read framing-playbook.md alongside this.*

---

## The mandate (say it to him verbatim)

> *"De Patrik hebt eusi Lead-DB suuber und macht eus jedi Wuche en churze Lead-Health-Digest für d Aakäller."*

He keeps the **sandbox test Leads DB** clean and produces a lightweight **weekly lead-health digest** for the cold-callers. It's his beat. From week 3 he owns a number.

## Why this surface (and not another)

- **Sandbox-safe.** It runs entirely on the throwaway test Leads DB — no live company data, no prod risk. He can own it fully *because* nothing he does there can break something real.
- **It composes the build curriculum into one coherent domain** he can hold in his head — every week's task feeds the same surface, so he's building *his thing*, not disconnected exercises.
- **It yields a real KPI number** — founder-hours saved on lead hygiene + hot-leads surfaced. Owning a number is what makes it feel like a job, not homework.

## How it composes the weeks

| Week | What he does to the surface |
|---|---|
| W3 | Cleans the test Leads DB **by hand** (dedupe, normalize, flag missing websites). First touch. |
| W5 | Builds the **normalizer tool** (his first from-scratch artifact) — automates part of the W3 hand-work. |
| W6 | Builds the **digest automation** — the weekly lead-health summary itself, from a scoped brief. |
| W7 | Keeps it **healthy under friction** — a seeded break he diagnoses + fixes. |
| W8 | **Runs his surface weekly, unprompted, self-verified** — the graduation proof. |

## The KPI (tie it to our buckets)

- **Bucket: less-cost** → founder-hours saved on lead hygiene per week.
- **Bucket: more-customers** → hot leads surfaced / not-lost because the DB was clean.
- **The number he owns:** "diä Wuche: X Dublette entfernt, Y Zeile mit fählende Date gflaggt, Z heissi Leads füre ghobe." He reports *his* number at Friday Show-&-Tell.

## The expert dynamic (from ~W5 — do this deliberately)

Once he owns it, **route the other founders' lead-DB questions to him** in the shared chat:
> *"Frag am beschte de Patrik, er hät de Lead-Stand am beschte druf."*

Being depended-on is worth more than praise. Do it in front of the others.

## The one guardrail that must not break

**Never take the surface back when he slips.** If a week is thin or he stalls, the response is *always* a **smaller next block on the same surface** — never "i mach de Digest da die Wuche sälber" or reassigning it to Tej. The moment it stops being his, the entire motivation engine dies. Shrink, don't reclaim.

## Confirm or swap (your call before W1)

The Lead-Ops Digest is the **recommended** surface — sandbox-safe, composable, real KPI. But the surface just needs those three properties; if another fits Patrik's interests better, swap it and the week-mapping still holds. Candidates:

- **LinkedIn-voice calibration** — he owns getting his own (+ maybe the team's) LinkedIn voice calibrated (he currently has *zero* writing samples on file). Real, low-stakes, composable. Weaker on the "KPI number" axis.
- **Walk-in Knowledge Base hygiene** — owns keeping the walk-in KB clean + summarized. Sandbox-safe if pointed at a test copy.
- **An internal weekly digest** (e.g. a "what shipped this week" summary for the founders) — real, visible, but less of a data-skills builder.

**Default: Lead-Ops Digest.** Change it here if you want a different beat for him.
