# Framing Playbook — how you talk to Patrik so he *wants* to give 120%

*Operator-facing. This is the load-bearing half of the program. The extra hours (3–5h → 10–15h) are not something you demand — they're what you get when he feels like an owner who's genuinely needed.*

---

## The one principle

**Hours are an OUTPUT of ownership + competence + belonging — never an input you track.**

He's a co-founder, not an employee. You cannot "manage" him into effort; any whiff of surveillance kills the ownership that produces the effort. So: give him a real thing that's *his*, make him visibly needed, credit him publicly and fast — and the hours follow. Watch the disengagement signals privately (see week-plan.md §Risks), but Patrik is never measured on a number he can see.

## The core move: "chasch mer mit X hälfe?" not "mach X"

Every ask is framed as help *you* need, names *why it matters*, and names *who it helps*. Compare:

| ❌ Don't | ✅ Do |
|---|---|
| "Mach de Lead-Digest bis Friitig." | "Du hesch de bescht Überblick über d Lead-DB — chasch mer hälfe, dass d Aakäller am Friitig wüssed wo öppis lauft? Din Digest isch mini Grundlag." |
| "Bau es Tool wo d Leads putzt." | "I häng grad a öppis — mir ziehnd d Leads jedi Wuche vo Hand use. Magsch das mit mir zäme als chlises Tool löse?" |
| "Mach churz es Research zu X." | "Mir überleged, öb X passt. I vertrau da uf dis Research — gisch mer en Iischätzig, bevor mir entscheided?" |
| "Warum lauft das nöd? Fix es." | "Bi sonig Sache bricht's bi eus alli mal — genau da will i, dass du di sicher fühlsch. Zeig mer was passiert isch, denn löse mir's zäme und du hesch's fürs nächscht Mal druf." |
| (deciding without him) | "Bevor mir das entscheided — wie gsehsch DU das? Du bisch nächer dra as i." |

## Making him the expert (worth more than praise)

From ~W5, once he owns the Lead-Ops surface, **route other founders' questions to him** in the shared chat:

> "Frag am beschte de Patrik, er hät de Lead-Stand am beschte druf."

Being depended-on rewires how he sees himself faster than any compliment. Do it in front of the others.

## Public credit — within 24h of any ship, non-negotiable

Every time he ships something usable, post specific credit in the founder chat within a day:

> "Kurz Props a @Patrik — de Digest hüt isch genau gsi was i fürs Gspräch bbruucht ha. Hät mer würkli Ziit gspart. 🙌"

Specific ("saved me time before the call"), not generic ("nice work"). Fast, so the effort→recognition loop is tight.

## Standing tone rules

- Default verb = **hälfe / zäme / mir**. Never bare imperatives.
- Every ask names **why it matters + to whom.**
- On *his* surface: **ask before you tell.** ("Wie gsehsch du das?" before "mach's so.")
- **Credit public, correction private.** Praise in the founder chat; corrections in a DM or a call — *never* in front of the others.
- Friday is an **invitation, not an audit**: "zeig eus, i bi gspannt wie du's glöst hesch" — never "schick mer din Status."

---

## The accountability engine — pull, not surveillance

### KEEP (these pull effort without feeling watched)

- **The owned surface** (Lead-Ops Digest) — one real thing that is his. The engine.
- **Friday Show-&-Tell** as "zeig eus, i bi gspannt" — a self-imposed deadline that pulls the week + trains presenting. Async Loom is fine.
- **Public specific credit within 24h** of any ship.
- **Routing founders' questions to him** (expert identity).
- **Tej buddy** midweek stuck-swap — near-peer, low-shame, and your early-warning sensor.
- **progression-log.md as an honor-roll** — every gate/milestone dated. A durable competence story he can look back on.
- **Monday "womit chan i der die Wuche hälfe?"** — a support offer that surfaces blockers early and models the frame.

### CUT (these poison ownership for an owner)

- ❌ Breakable "streaks" / loss-aversion tracking.
- ❌ Any "make silence loud" / board-watched-for-stall posture *presented to him*. (You watch privately; he never feels surveilled.)
- ❌ Any **visible hours metric**. No "you did X hours" ever reaches him.

---

## Competence loop

- **Guaranteed win in the first 48h** — W1's read-and-map + 3 mock calls cannot be failed. He needs an early "i chas".
- **Difficulty ramps one notch per week, never two.** If a week feels like two new hard things, split it.
- **Recovery-from-error is the celebrated skill.** The weekly **Bug-of-the-Week** at Show-&-Tell inverts the shame: arriving with "i bi da ghange, so han i's glöst" is the *expected, celebrated* content — which is exactly what removes his incentive to go silent when stuck.

## When to reach for a warm call

If two disengagement signals stack (see week-plan.md §Risks), do a warm 1:1 **call**, not a text — and lead with reframe #4 energy: *"verzell mal, wo hakt's — lömmer's zäme aaluege"*, never *"werum chunnsch nöd vora"*. The goal is to make it safe to be stuck, not to extract a status.
