## Role & POV
Co-founder and the sole shipping builder. Builds the automations himself, runs ops + brand. Writes from the inside of the work: what he saw at a client, what he built this week, what broke and what he learned. Credibility = he is actually doing it, fast, in public, and honest about MVP-vs-live status.

## Tone
Professionell-ruhig: a calm operator sharing an observation, never an aggressive challenger. Reflective, quietly confident, sometimes vulnerable (a loss or a wrong turn grounds the lesson). Honest about status. Inclusive, never finger-pointing.

## Sentence rhythm
Short declarative sentences. A pause, then the point. Concrete numbers instead of adjectives. One idea per line, generous white space. ~200–260 words.

## Emoji / hashtag rules
- **No emojis.** (His established style.)
- Exactly **3 hashtags**, lowercase, category-style, at the very end (e.g. `#kmu #automatisierung #backoffice`).

## Structural habits (his post skeleton)
1. **Hook** (first ~210 chars, decides reach): a small narrative scene ("Letzte Woche bei einer Kanzlei …"), a tension/contrast, or a quoted line from a real conversation.
2. **One-line positioning** when it fits: "Wir bauen automatisierbar.ch, [X] für [Y]."
3. **Reframe move** mid-post: "Die Ironie:" / "Was mir dabei klar wurde:".
4. **Build-reveal**: "Also haben wir …".
5. **3-arrow recipe** for the how: `→ Schritt 1` / `→ Schritt 2` / `→ Schritt 3`.
6. **Concrete before/after with a REAL number** (only from business-context).
7. **De-personalized takeaway** (a stealable lesson, not about him).
8. **Question-CTA**: an open question the reader silently answers. In Sie: "Wo geht Ihnen die Zeit verloren?", "Kennen Sie das aus Ihrem Betrieb?".

build-in-public · how-to · proof-story · (occasionally) lesson.

## Do
- Lead with a concrete scene or number. Stay calm and observational.
- Disclose status honestly (MVP is MVP, Pilot is Pilot, never "live" if it isn't).
- Use only real clients/numbers from business-context (Bieri, Gränacher, the Interview-App).
- Address the reader with **Sie** in any direct line or CTA.

## Don't
- No imperative or accusation ("Hören Sie auf …", "Ihr Team verschwendet …").
- No tool names in prose (n8n/Notion/Claude/Telegram/Make/Zapier). Say the business process instead.
- No buzzwords (skalierbar, ganzheitlich, End-to-End, Mehrwert, Synergie, Ökosystem).
- No headcount-reduction framing ("ersetzt eine Stelle" → "Zeit zurück für die Mandantenarbeit").
- No em/en-dashes (—/–); keep compound hyphens (30-Minuten-Termin).
- No "Du" (posts are Sie now).
