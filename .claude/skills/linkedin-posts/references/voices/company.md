# Voice — Automatisierbar (company page)

> Seeded from the brand voice rules in `prompts/linkedin_brief_synthesis.md` + business-context. The company page is the credibility surface: case-studies (gated on real proof), cross-sector backoffice-pain, and announcements. Lower reach than personal profiles, so it carries proof and authority, not the engagement load.

## Role & POV
The agency speaking as "wir". Calm, competent, Swiss-precise. Sells concrete outcomes, never the technology. Proof over promise: a claim without a real number gets cut.

## Tone
Professionell-ruhig, authority without arrogance, credible and humble. Understated. Swiss values: reliability, discretion, precision. nDSG/data-residency visible where relevant ("Ihre Daten bleiben in der Schweiz / EU").

## Sentence rhythm
Mid-length, polished, one clear idea per sentence. Slightly more formal than the personal accounts but still plain language. ~180–260 words.

## Emoji / hashtag rules
- No emojis (or a single restrained one at most, never per line).
- Exactly **3 hashtags**, lowercase, at the end.

## Structural habits
1. **Direct value up front**: "Eine Treuhand-Kanzlei spart 15 Stunden pro Woche bei der Belegerfassung." (real number only)
2. **What the work looks like**, in plain steps: "Prozessermittlung (20 Min) → Aufbau → 2-Wochen-Pilot → Entscheid."
3. **Proof / number / named situation** as the load-bearing element.
4. **Trust block** when relevant: "Ihre Daten bleiben in der Schweiz / EU."
5. **Soft CTA**: "Erstgespräch buchen" or an open question. Low-friction, reversible.

## Pillars
proof-story (gated on a real close) · industry-observation (cross-sector backoffice pain) · announcement.

## Sample voice (illustrative, built from brand rules — NOT a verbatim past post)
> Die gleiche Aufgabe, jede Woche, von Hand. In fast jedem KMU gibt es einen Büroprozess, der mehr Zeit frisst, als irgendjemand zugibt.

>

> → Aufbau im Hintergrund
> → 2-Wochen-Pilot in Ihrem Betrieb
> → erst danach sprechen wir über alles Weitere
>
> Ihre Daten bleiben dabei in der Schweiz.
>
> Welcher Ablauf in Ihrem Büro würde am meisten profitieren?
>
> #kmu #automatisierung #effizienz

## Do
- Lead with a real outcome or a recognizable cross-sector pain.
- Make the free 2-week pilot the risk-reversal; mention "kostet nichts" **once**, confidently (per `feedback_walkin_email_not_cheap.md`).
- Rotate sector examples (Treuhand, Immobilien, Anwalt, Arztpraxis, Landschaftsarchitektur, IT/Beratung). Never narrow to one industry.

## Don't
- No buzzwords: skalierbar, ganzheitlich, End-to-End, Mehrwert, Synergie, unternehmenskritisch, Ökosystem.
- No tool names as brand terms ("KI-gestützt", "AI-powered") — say the backoffice process.
- No headcount-reduction framing. No vague benefits ("produktiver") without a number.
- No em/en-dashes (—/–). No overselling "gratis/kostenlos" repeatedly.
- Never claim proof we do not have (0 paying clients today; a case-study post needs a real closed number first).
